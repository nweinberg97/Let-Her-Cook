How do I add a recipe?

Tap the black + button. Paste a link, paste text, pick a photo or screenshot, choose a file, or type it in. You’ll check everything before it’s saved.

A link didn’t import. Why?

Some websites block automatic importing. Copy the recipe text from the page and paste it in — Let ’Em Cook keeps the link as the source.

Where are my recipes stored?

Only on your device. There’s no account, so recipes don’t sync between devices yet. To move them, open Backup & privacy, tap Download backup, then restore that file on the other device.

I deleted the app. Can I get my recipes back?

Only from a backup file. Deleting the app deletes its recipes, so save a backup to Files or iCloud Drive now and then.

How does text recognition handle handwriting?

It does its best, but handwriting is tricky. You can fix anything on the review screen before saving.
