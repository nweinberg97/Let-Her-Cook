Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version: Let ’Em Cook doesn’t collect, store or share your personal data. Your recipes live on your device, and we never see them.

What stays on your device

Everything you add — recipes, photos you choose to keep, collections, tags, favorites and settings — is saved only on your device, inside the app (or your browser, if you use the website). There are no accounts and no Let ’Em Cook servers holding your recipes.

Importing recipes

  • Photos, screenshots, PDFs and documents are read on your device, including text recognition. They are not uploaded anywhere, and the original file isn’t kept after the import.
  • Links: when you import a link, your device requests that web page directly from the website, just like opening it. We don’t receive the link or the page.
  • Camera and photo access is only used when you choose to import or add a photo.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there’s nothing to sell.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

Sharing and backups

When you share a recipe or save a backup, the app hands it to your device’s share sheet, and you choose where it goes (Messages, Files, iCloud Drive, email and so on). Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Use “Delete everything” on the Backup & privacy screen, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored by us, there’s nothing else to delete.

Children

The app doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, if optional syncing between devices is added — we’ll update this page and explain the change in the app before anything new happens.
